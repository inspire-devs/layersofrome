Dr. Ronald J. Weber

Dr. Ronald J. Weber

Interim Chair of the Philosophy Department

Director of the Humanities Program & the Masters of Arts in Interdisciplinary Studies Program at The University of Texas at El Paso

Associate Professor of Ancient History and Humanities, I currently serve as the interim chair of the Philosophy Department and Director of the Humanities Program and Masters of Arts in Interdisciplinary Studies Programs at the University of Texas at El Paso. My research interests focus on the history of ancient Rome, Roman architecture, subterranean stratigraphy, and digital humanities.

Since founding the Layers of Rome project nearly two decades ago, I have led faculty and students on annual field expeditions to Italy, investigating ancient sites from the Forum and Suburra to Ostia Antica. Through NEH grants and interdisciplinary collaboration, our mission is to create accessible, open-source educational resources that bring the rich multi-layered heritage of Rome to educators and students worldwide.
