# Content migration checklist

The initial site structure follows the Layers of Rome Website Bible. These items require verified source content or current program decisions:

- Replace display-only architectural artwork with approved documentary photography where appropriate.
- Add complete faculty and staff profiles, portraits, credits, and current titles.
- Restore every Student Journal quotation verbatim with attribution and photography.
- Confirm current Study Abroad dates, fees, application links, housing, scholarships, and passport guidance.
- Add the current syllabus and calendar.
- Select and configure the Study Abroad map provider.
- Restore the full text, standards, downloads, videos, and author credits for every lesson plan.
- Inventory all PDFs, PowerPoints, postcards, and other downloadable resources.
- Restore the four Preserving Identities digital exhibits and their media.
- Rebuild the Community Outreach timeline with verified dates, descriptions, and photographs.
- Select a provider/data source for Ostia Antica maps and the Roman Timeline.
- Confirm the Class Folder URL and whether it should remain in public navigation.
- Add complete image alt text, copyright/credit, focal point, and usage rights metadata.
- Verify UTEP-required institutional footer links, identity rules, accessibility link, privacy link, and site-feedback destination.
- Self-host the selected fonts or replace them with approved institutional fonts to remove the Google Fonts runtime request.
