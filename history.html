About the Layers of Rome Project

The Layers of Rome project is an interdisciplinary initiative focused on bringing the history, archaeology, and urban evolution of Ancient Rome to students, researchers, and global audiences.

Subterranean Basilicas

Analyzing three-tiered sites like San Clemente where pagan temples lie beneath 4th-century basilicas and medieval structures.

Preserving Urban Shrines

Documenting small neighborhood shrines (compita) and street altars across classical and post-classical Rome.

Digital Archaeology

Employing 3D scanning, spatial GIS, and photographic reconstruction to preserve vulnerable Roman heritage.
