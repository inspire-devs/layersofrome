John De Frank

Lecturer in Humanities & History

Co-Director for the Layers of Rome Program at The University of Texas at El Paso

John De Frank is a dedicated historian and lecturer in the Department of History and Humanities Program at the University of Texas at El Paso. As Co-Director of the Layers of Rome project, he specializes in curriculum design, student field mentorship, and digital archiving of Roman historical sites.

With extensive experience guiding study abroad programs across Italy, John works closely with undergraduate and graduate students to translate field research, photogrammetry, and spatial analysis into interactive online modules for educators worldwide.
