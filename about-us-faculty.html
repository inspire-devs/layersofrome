Contributing Faculty & Staff

The success of the Layers of Rome project is made possible through the interdisciplinary contributions of faculty, researchers, technical specialists, and student scholars across History, Humanities, Communication, and Computer Science at UTEP.

NEH Summer Scholars

25 selected educators who developed lesson plans for Roman monuments in English culture during the NEH Summer Institute.

Digital Media & GIS Team

Specialists managing 3D scanning, spatial map tours, photogrammetry, and web accessibility features.

Student Researchers

UTEP study abroad participants who conduct on-site field research in Rome and Ostia Antica.
